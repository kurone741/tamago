# tamago

tamago is a simple desktop companion. I built it because I thought it seemed like a good entry point into pcb design, without being unnecessarily complex. 

Functionality includes date/time, timer, stopwatch, countdown, and noise visualizer which responds to the sounds in your working space (e.g. typing/music). It can also display humidity and temperature of your working space. It's a device that is for the most part single-purpose, meaning it doesn't occupy more of your attention than it needs. THis also helps me stay on focus more.

ps. tamago is japanese for egg
